Contact Us
Do I need to tip?
Tipping is always appreciated but never expected. Our barbers earn a competitive wage — tip only if you feel the service exceeded expectations.
Do you accept walk-ins?
Absolutely! Walk-ins are welcome any time during business hours. During peak hours (Fri–Sat) we recommend booking ahead to guarantee your preferred barber.
What's your cancellation policy?
We ask for at least 4 hours notice for cancellations. Late cancellations may incur a 50% fee to protect our barbers' time. No-shows are charged in full.
Can I pay with card or only cash?
We accept all major credit/debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and good old cash. No surcharges on card payments.
Do membership plans auto-renew?
Yes, plans auto-renew monthly. You can cancel anytime with no penalty — we'll complete your current month and cancel the next renewal. No lock-in contracts.
