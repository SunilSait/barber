Who We Are

A Barbershop Built on Brotherhood & Craft

BarberCo was born in 2012 out of a simple belief: every man deserves a great barbershop experience. Not just a quick trim, but a ritual — a moment to slow down, sit back, and leave looking and feeling like the best version of himself.

Co-founded by Marcus Johnson and James Carter, two barbers who met as apprentices and shared a vision for something greater, BarberCo has grown from a two-chair shop into downtown's most trusted grooming destination.

Our Journey

Built Over
a Decade of Dedication

From two chairs and a dream to downtown's most trusted grooming studio — here are the milestones that shaped us.

2012
BarberCo Opens Its Doors
Marcus and James open a two-chair shop on Main Street with nothing but talent, two barber chairs, and an ambitious vision.
2015
Expansion & Daniel Joins
BarberCo doubles in size. Daniel Reyes joins as Senior Barber, bringing his legendary beard grooming skills to the team.
2019
Best Barbershop Award
Voted Best Barbershop in the city by Downtown Style Magazine. 5,000+ clients served milestone reached.
2022
Premium Services Launch
Launched the VIP grooming packages and online booking system. Revenue grew by 60% in one year.
2024
City Style Award & 8,500 Clients
Won the City Style Award for Best Men's Grooming. BarberCo now serves over 8,500 satisfied clients.
